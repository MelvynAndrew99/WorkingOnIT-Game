# Working ON IT! — Credits

**Fix the commute. Take the credit.**

From idea to a playable jam game in roughly seven days. Open **Credits** on the game’s main menu to read these contributions while playing.

The Man might take all the credit in the game. Here is how the work was actually shared.

## Design & creative direction — Melvyn Andrew

Melvyn Andrew brought the vision, selected and developed ideas, directed the tools, played the results, and decided what belonged in the game. Working ON IT! grew through that cycle of experimentation, feedback, and revision.

## AI-assisted contributions

These credits reflect the creator's account of how each tool contributed. Roles overlapped as features were reviewed, corrected, and integrated.

| Tool | Contributions |
| --- | --- |
| **Grok** | Menus and UI layout, artwork for the main title, game balance, and verification of game systems. |
| **Claude** | Sprite artwork and corrections to awkward or troublesome UI interfaces. |
| **Codex** | Managing and coordinating agents, solving complex system problems including performance, and implementing new game elements. |
| **Suno** | Music, generated through the creator's music workflow and supplied for integration into the game. |
| **Gemini** | Idea exploration that helped shape traffic problems into the game's mission format. |

Balance work during development helped shape the jam game; the wider economy remains provisional, with deeper balancing planned after the jam.

## How the collaboration worked

Ideas became design decisions, scoped tasks, playable changes, and checks against the actual game. Different tools helped with different problems, while the creator retained creative direction and final acceptance.

The [project instructions](AGENTS.md), [design documents](docs/DESIGN.md), and [implementation lessons](docs/IMPLEMENTATION-LESSONS.md) preserve that context for future contributors. Individual delivery records describe specific assignments, integration changes, and verification; the credits above summarize the broader roles.

Pull requests and human review remain part of that process. Contributors are responsible for understanding their changes, checking the results, and explaining the reasoning, including when AI helped produce the work. See [contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Foundation and acknowledgements

Working ON IT! builds on the **RUN.world game-jam starter**, using **TypeScript, PixiJS, React, Vite, and Tailwind CSS**, with **Nix** providing the development environment.

Thanks to the players who tried the game and shared confusing moments, traffic problems, and ideas for improvement. That feedback helped shape both fixes and new lessons.

Template terms are recorded in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt). Asset-specific provenance and production notes remain with their respective files and delivery records, including the [sprite work](docs/apartments/README.md), [audio brief](docs/AUDIO-BRIEF.md), and [radio recordings](public/audio/radio/README.md).
