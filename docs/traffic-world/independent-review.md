# Independent Codex review — proposals only

Delivered by the traffic_world_review agent after reading shared implementation lessons and the bounded brief. Grok did not deliver a review in its two capped attempts; do not attribute these ideas to Grok.

The plan's strongest choice is reusing a persistent district to demonstrate measurable improvement. Richness should come first from visible journeys, queues and recovery.

Smallest reusable visual kit:
- Existing building and vehicle atlas; small vehicle-purpose badges where needed.
- Code-drawn route arrows, queue bands, entrance markers and destination-capacity badges.
- One shared intersection marker/panel for phase, approach waiting and selected route.
- Cyan selection/routes, amber waiting, red incidents; pair colors with shapes or text. Decoration stays quieter than traffic.

Avoid new buildings, decorative road variants and inactive upgrade controls in the first comparison. An empty upgrade area consumes phone space and suggests functionality.

Sequence: actual responder recovery baseline; junction comparison at equal demand counting returns/unmet demand; overlay showing queue/disconnection/full destination; six explicit data presets; one-way/roundabout with directed returns and save migration; verified multi-lane merges before later tools. Establish measurement before presentation. Adaptive routing can move a bottleneck, so averages alone are insufficient.

One mockup: equal-scale before/after district views, first a queue with available shop capacity, then a player-built bypass. Show matching demand/window labels, completed returns, longest wait and unserved households. Add a small full-shop inset and phone-width crop. Mark illustrative numbers clearly rather than invent measured improvement.

Shared scenario schema: schedule, purpose mix, origins/destinations, capacity and optional event. Morning peaks outbound; evening returning; weekend leisure; festival finite concentrated visits; accident stress optional explicit incident; optimized flow changes infrastructure at baseline demand.

Proposed lesson: establish visual meaning from actual simulation states before multiplying assets. Verify that capacity, access and movement delay remain distinguishable at phone scale.

Lead integration: accepted measurement-first sequencing, minimal kit, phone crop, and removal of an empty upgrade placeholder. These remain proposed next deliverables, not implemented features or user-approved new artwork.
