# UI-06 — remove Jobs & city link clutter

Status: implemented and verified locally September 10, 2026. No publication. UI-01–05 remain completed historical work; this explicit cleanup supersedes their requirement to keep the full Jobs/city-link reference screens reachable.

## User decision

Remove the persistent “Jobs & city link” button and its Jobs and City link & guide screens. The user finds lists of already-completed jobs/levels and finished lessons clutter, and does not need a separate screen stating the gateway coordinates or tutorial completion. Keep attention on the current task. Do not create a replacement archive, move the completed list elsewhere or redesign unrelated UI.

## Implementation scope

- Remove the shared entry point, emergency-detail Jobs shortcut, dialog/tab wiring and obsolete components/styles/imports that become genuinely unused. Preserve the user's other uncommitted changes.
- Keep the current mission card, active progress, reward and normal next-job/land progression. An earned reward awaiting claim is still actionable: never hide it just because its objective is done. Preserve exactly-once claims and all saved completion/claim/permit receipts.
- Preserve active tutorial teaching, tool/category highlights, Details, Skip confirmation, final expansion and explicit finish/outside-traffic invitation. Remove stale copy directing players into Jobs or City link & guide.
- Audit older completed/skipped/disconnected saves and pending automatic-connection requests. Ensure the needed finish/connect action and a truthful pending-connection explanation remain available on the current objective when applicable; do not depend on the removed sheet, auto-connect without consent, or restart completed tutorials. An established gateway keeps working; removing its coordinate display does not remove outside traffic.
- The historical lesson review/resume catalogue is part of the removed clutter. Do not reintroduce it through a new permanent control. Preserve any genuinely needed active/available tutorial entry through the current objective.
- Leave other controls, simulation, cities, artwork/audio, camera focus/input and saved display preferences intact. No publishing.

However, TutorialPanel currently also handles older disconnected/pending connection cases: audit those before deletion. `src/game/cityStarterTutorial.ts` still has a hint directing players to City link & guide from Jobs; correct presentation copy without changing progression rules.
