# From a generated picture to AI Overlord cover art

Approximate runtime: 90 seconds. Show the actual saved candidates in chronological order; do not invent model responses or imply a single prompt produced the result.

[Show the first glossy robot candidates.]
We started with an AI game about replacing human workers. Our first cover images looked polished, but I would have scrolled straight past them. They showed a robot. They did not sell the game.

[Show the more serious scenes.]
I said I wanted the feeling of SNES, PlayStation and Nintendo 64 box art. That needed clarification: I meant the composition and personality that made you pick up a box in a store. I wasn't asking for old graphics.

[Show a calm face against disaster, then our original robot interpretation.]
The breakthrough was comedy. Think of someone looking completely pleased with themselves while everything behind them falls apart. That contrast reads even in a tiny thumbnail. We used the idea behind familiar memes, with our own character and setting.

[Show the low-angle city cover and expression iteration.]
Then we borrowed a presentation principle from game box art: look up at one strong character, give the title room, and let the background suggest a bigger world. No borrowed weapon, character or franchise theme.

The robot couldn't look obviously evil. Before playing, that smug expression should feel like an AI confidently getting things wrong. After mastering the game, you might look again and wonder: did it choose this?

[Show the accepted cover at full size, then thumbnail size.]
Grok made the visual candidates. Codex helped coordinate the iterations, and Claude contributed another perspective. But the useful progress came from explaining why an image missed the feeling, and recording those decisions.

This is our current accepted cover, not a promise that we will never change it. The lesson: don't just ask AI for a better picture. Tell it what the player should feel, what they should notice first, and what the image should mean after they play.
