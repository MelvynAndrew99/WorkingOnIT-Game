The v0.1 tutorial and traffic update for Working ON IT!

- Build your town on a guided H-shaped road layout, with highlighted tools and player-chosen growth locations.
- Handle a visible crash while traffic keeps running. Cars approach blockages and queue, then continue toward their destinations when a bypass opens.
- Connect both ends of the H, learn optional road diversions, and send EMS from a Clinic to clear the tutorial crash.
- See missing road access and required emergency responders directly on the map. The manager offers delayed advice when a problem remains unresolved.
- Finish the tutorial with two free map expansions, then earn more land through growth missions.
- Stops cost $25 and Lights cost $75. Adjusting signal timing is free, and removing purchased controls refunds their actual cost.
- Improved phone and desktop guidance, saved progress, and emergency access.

Music integration and deeper traffic mechanics are planned for the next development milestone.
