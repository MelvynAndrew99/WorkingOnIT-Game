# Small lessons, better roads

- Enter Challenges from the main menu and follow the numbered training route.
- Connect a home to a store, serve a neighborhood, and learn to manage a crossing safely. The original traffic-flow puzzle remains a bonus.
- Earn saved completion stars, retry a level or move straight to the next.
- Play/Pause and Reset sit together at the bottom-right of the map. Reset keeps earned stars and your sandbox town.
- Background music loops throughout Challenges, including planning and victory screens.
- Challenge budgets stay fixed while your sandbox keeps its normal economy.
- Includes improved neighborhood-flow feedback and traffic recovery fixes.
