**Yes — this is the real game.** Service and growth goals as a whole-game “keep the town running” loop is stronger than a first-puzzle tutorial. Fun over accuracy holds: exaggerate jams and recoveries, keep cause and effect readable. Saved towns, receipts, no forced fail if the plan was sound, no random accidents to unsolve traffic, no reset-deadlock, no stamped layout. Manager can help for real and then take the credit; comic timing, not cruelty. We are recording a direction, not shipping it.

**Appeal.** Self-inflicted disruption is better than disasters. You *choose* to upgrade, you *choose* when, and the town has to live through it. That is field-flavored without inventing new failure machines. A detour is not a rescue: it only moves demand. If the remaining path or the remaining shop is already the bottleneck, Divert just relocates the queue.

**Three different redundancies.** Do not treat them as one slider.

- **Road redundancy:** a second *path*. Survives a diverted segment.
- **Destination redundancy:** a second *store or park* that can take the same trip type. Survives one place being full or unreachable.
- **Spare capacity:** extra slots or throughput on the *same* road or destination. Absorbs a peak; it is not a second road.

A neighborhood on one road, or one shop, or one park, is a single point of failure. Widening the only road buys spare capacity, not a second path. Building a parallel road buys road redundancy. A second supermarket buys destination redundancy. Players should be able to mix those; do not stamp a “correct” grid.

**Divert vs hard closure.** Today Divert is a traffic ban: civilians and ordinary returning crews stay off selected tiles; outbound responders may cross. It is **not** an excavated trench. For upgrades, reuse that idea: route ordinary traffic around works, **do not** invent a physical hole. Hard closure would strand districts and fight the no-deadlock rule. Outbound police/EMS/fire still have to reach the affected area, with existing incident deadlines. Returning crews still obey Divert. That is enough emergency access until someone explicitly designs temporary lanes or crews — those do **not** exist now.

**Smallest fun loop.** Pick a pinch (one-lane corridor or undersized destination) → **preview** who is banned, remaining paths, responder access, that extra lanes are not live yet → **drain** so you do not trap a jam on the works tiles → **start** (Divert-like restriction + sim-time wait) → live with detour congestion and service/growth pressure → **reopen** with the upgrade actually in force. Ordinary new construction can stay instant; only upgrade *timers* are on the table.

**Timer.** Simulation time, not wall-clock, not offline. Pause pauses works (otherwise pause is a skip). Playing the rest of the town *is* the non-idle wait. No arbitrary deadlines, upkeep, random disasters, or paid skips. **Durations and penalties: unselected** — do not pick numbers yet.

**Fair failure, not cruelty.** Diverting the only road into a neighborhood is a player-made outage: shopping/leisure/returns starve, goals slip, responders can still go in. Stacking two upgrades onto one remaining street is sequencing, not RNG. A fat detour onto a thin residential is detour congestion. Those are readable. Unfair: invalidating a solved network with a dice-roll crash, or a lock that only a reset breaks.

**Cancel / recover without exploits.** There is no hole to “leave open.” Cancel should not instant-reopen as if the upgrade finished, and should not be a flicker-Divert cheat. After a commit-past-drain, cancel restores the *old* road after a short restore/drain, no bonus lanes, no infinite retry that deletes the wait. Exact restore length: unselected.

**Creative alternatives stay legal.** Second road, second shop, junction timing, Divert without upgrading, or just live with the pinch. Do not force widening.

**Order (previous choice still right).** 1) Existing-tool flow prototype (1+1 lanes, junctions, Divert). 2) **Real** four-lane widening — extra lanes, merges, junctions that actually carry more. Gate timed roadworks on that; a timer whose payoff is still a painted 1+1 is a wait for a lie. 3) Then preview/drain/start/reopen around upgrades. Field-inspired challenges after those verbs exist. Not implementing now.
