# Interface and missions: collaboration decisions

September 9, 2026. User requested Codex, installed Claude Code and installed Grok Build. Their actual independent responses are preserved in claude-review.md and grok-review.md. This is a bounded first playable, not verified balance or a full tutorial/campaign.

- User intent: growth and thrift first; experimentation before later efficiency/safety objectives. Manager urges destinations/traffic and deferred service spending, recommending bypasses during incidents. Retain this shortsighted voice alongside factual dispatch/deadline information.
- Both reviewers identify $10,000 and $100/visit as generous enough that service poverty is not currently a meaningful constraint. Lead retains funds/costs/income and full refunds, uses recognition rather than cash payouts, and reports budget expenditure without penalizing early services. Smaller future-new-town grants require deliberate later tuning after playtests.
- Claude proposes all buildings visible; Grok proposes Town/Civic/Traffic categories. Lead is testing Claude's direct-access grid with compact stats, measured on phones. It must preserve useful map space and readable targets. If it fails those checks, reduce the palette with named categories instead of shrinking text.
- Grok correctly calls for unique current households served; lead adopts that durable metric. Early arc: first real shopping visit, three households shopping, three households visiting a park, then six households shopping. No wait limit, trips-per-minute gate, forced accident or on-road occupancy target. Claude's early throughput targets and Grok's peak active-car target are omitted because user wants efficiency later. Redundant stores and stranded vehicles cannot win these goals.
- Grok's optional Thrifty badge for having no services is not adopted: foresight and early protection should not cost recognition. Budget advice remains character voice and a visible construction-cost breakdown.
- Grok's claim that one-car-per-home cannot jam this map is unverified and conflicts with existing measured queue/starvation tests. Congestion can emerge from layouts now; outside traffic remains a later growth mechanism, not silently added here.
- Incidents surface context-sensitive detour/service guidance, never a requirement to cause a crash. Bypasses do not complete rescue objectives or clear wrecks. User's manager can give bad budget advice; a distinct crew line reports actual unmet services/deadlines. Any alert is optional guidance, not a tool gate.
- Missions can be hidden and reopened. Completion persists and recognizes work done ahead of sequence. Saved counters start from observed visits after this update; existing layouts, funds, trips and old namespaces are preserved. No retroactive made-up visit history.

## User correction after the reviews

The user explicitly rejected treating tutorial-stage local caps, generous construction, absent outside traffic and parked visits as permanent main-game limitations. Road optimization is the main game; external cities add demand as town growth and supported capacity increase. Parked visitors free road capacity for additional traffic and concentrate arrivals/departures. Bus stops, wider roads and one-way roads are intended future tools; later efficiency incentives are needed. Current interface/first missions prepare this progression, not a complete balance model. Claude and Grok were sent this correction in traffic-first-correction.txt. Their earlier responses are preserved as historical review evidence and superseded where they conflict.

## Staged tutorial and free assistance (user clarification, 2026-09-09)

Tutorial scenarios may deliberately create small, legible traffic problems so players learn to recognize them and experiment with tools. Provide an optional free worked solution when a player gets stuck; assistance should explain the change and let the real simulation demonstrate its result. This supersedes any blanket prohibition on staged tutorial problems. Preserve creative alternative solutions and successful foresight. Main-game pressure still comes from growth; do not force every successful layout to fail. Exact scenarios and the assistance interaction remain to be implemented and verified. The current optional growth mission board is a foundation, not the completed tutorial, external-city transition, or free solution system.
