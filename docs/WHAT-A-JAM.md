# What a Jam!

User-supplied ending-credits song for **Working ON IT! — Fix the commute. Take the credit.**, September 9, 2026. Lyrics and style below are preserved as supplied. This records the creative brief; no audio recording has been supplied or installed.

The song is the city manager's personal victory speech: joyful, self-satisfied, and sincerely pleased to help. Helping people is the real gameplay achievement; his appetite for credit at the expense of other people's pain is the joke. He welcomes their misery because it creates his opportunity to be needed and celebrated, without recognizing that selfishness. His selfishness is a lack of self-awareness, not malicious or villainous: he cannot read the room and does not notice how his excitement about being needed lands with people in pain. He does not wish them harm or deliberately create suffering for credit. See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for the accepted game tone and [AUDIO-BRIEF.md](AUDIO-BRIEF.md) for production context.
