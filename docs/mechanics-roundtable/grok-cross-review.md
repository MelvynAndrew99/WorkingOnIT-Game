**Shared contract (explicit).** All figures stay provisional. Starting cash is **900**; there is no new-town building. Shopping pays **once at a completed visit**. The return leg does not pay and must not be retimed: return **frees the car** for the next trip; the destination **slot frees on departure**, not on home arrival. Track **return, coverage, and safety separately** from revenue. Both outbound and return remain mandatory for a served household. Parks stay **real leisure destinations**: base **20/10s** recovery plus **15/10s per recently served household**. Do not convert them into home multipliers, shut them off, or “fix” idle income before a measured review. A park never absorbs a shopping trip.

Demand is a queue of **typed household trips** (shop, leisure; work/school later), not an RCI bar. Count **unspawned and blocked** demand, not only admitted cars. If a trip cannot spawn, log an **unserved reason** (`no path` / `no capacity`; do not invent `too far` until a cutoff is an explicit, tested rule). Destination choice among buildings of the right class with free capacity: **lowest actual travel time**. That is routing, not a payout rule. **No hidden efficiency multiplier, spend decay, or distance bonus.** Shorter cycles already earn more visits/minute; that physical throughput is the proximity reward.

The city **keeps running**. AFK online production is legitimate. Do not pause the sim on crash, freeze demand, or require click-to-reaccept trips. Manager advice is optional; it does not take the wheel. Growth is **player-chosen**. Early friction **delays extra earnings**; it does not levy ordinary upkeep or extra congestion fines in the first slice. Occupied space is the scarce resource. Full construction refunds stay. Place–earn–refund is **not** an exploit. Exploits are **duplicated demand/receipts** and **reset farming**. Reservation leaks, orphaned trips on demolish, and entitlement double-spend stay real bugs: replan or refuse; never delete a destination under a live journey.

**First delivery, in order:** (1) goal-preservation audit so metrics cannot rescue the wrong destination; (2) visible unserved reasons; (3) **one** non-timed shared-junction growth objective with a small claimable reward. Then a **capacity** puzzle (road ≠ store slots), then **bounded** outside flow, then **opt-in occupancy** inside the existing footprint. **No new tools** (wider/one-way, upgrade trees, new currencies, disaster economy) until current roads, stops, and lights have two materially different solutions under the same demand/incident seed. Stops and lights remain basic safety tools; signals are not assumed higher throughput.

**Agree / disagree.** Agree with Codex on growth-by-choice, visit-timed pay, counting blocked demand, neighborhood coverage that cannot be sacrificed to averages, and diagnostics-before-systems. Agree that completed visits/minute and income/minute plus unserved, return access, and local wait are the honest board. Disagree with Claude on parks-as-amplifiers, revenue-on-round-trip, travel-time **spend** decay, early building levels, decaying refunds after first visit, and treating earn-then-refund as a pump. Clash-like per-building levels are a later opt-in density spine, **after** occupancy and current-tool depth; downgrade under the same refund rule if they ship. Incident-scoped, locally recoverable timers only; no global clock in this slice. Police/fire are not first-slice demand or cost surfaces. Newtown-vs-clinic is a false question: **900 is cash, not a building**.

Visit income **already** rewards proximity via cycle time; a second multiplier would double-pay compact layouts and hide the road puzzle. Unlimited park/recovery plus shopping can dominate AFK—that is a **balance measurement**, not a reason to kill idle production or park destinations. First growth unlock is **not** occupancy and **not** a new trip purpose; it is a second neighborhood through a shared junction, with occupancy later so space matters without mandatory demolition.

**Isolated pods.** Do not forbid a home+shop cul-de-sac; it is a valid early rest. Stop it from being the forever-optimum **without a compactness score or isolation tax**: the shared-junction objective (and later bounded outside arrivals) puts conflicting journeys on one crossing so a second store, a short controlled junction, or a perimeter/loop are alternatives to “another sealed pod.” Score **completed visits and unserved by neighborhood**, not city averages. Optional road-tile comparisons may motivate a loop; a long bypass that actually clears conflict still wins. Do not force an accident quota.

**No-space / zero-cash fallback.** No-growth remains stable and unpunished. Full refunds are the unwind: sell geometry, recover cash and tiles, replan. Never gate the next objective on buying land if reorganization of owned tiles can serve it. If the map is full, the exits are refund-and-rebuild, occupancy **only after** that alternative is proven, or sit on production. If cash is zero, production continues; the player is not required to spend. Tests: same demand seed, warmup, several cycles; a growing starved approach fails even if total throughput rises.
